# Vibgyor Print N Pack — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Material Intelligence
Very brief intro: A tactile, editorial B2B direction that treats packaging films and pouches like engineered materials in a curated archive. Warm whites, cobalt, saffron, graphite, and precise typographic rhythm create premium confidence without looking corporate-generic.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Factory After Hours
Very brief intro: A dark, cinematic industrial direction built around machinery, rollers, and reflective laminate surfaces. High-contrast visuals and electric color accents make the manufacturing process feel contemporary and energetic.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Quiet Precision
Very brief intro: A clean, restrained corporate direction where generous space, cool neutrals, and sharp diagrams signal quality systems and dependable execution. The visual language is calm, clear, and procurement-friendly.
Probability: 0.02

## Chosen approach: Material Intelligence

### Design Movement
Contemporary Swiss editorial design fused with industrial material culture: modular systems, deliberate asymmetry, clear information hierarchy, and tactile close-ups of engineered surfaces.

### Core Principles
1. Make the material the hero: film, foil, laminate sheen, roller lines, and pouch geometry carry the visual story.
2. Build trust through evidence: show the converting journey, structural options, quality checkpoints, and application detail.
3. Use asymmetry with control: offset columns, split compositions, and editorial numbering create a crafted feel without sacrificing scanability.
4. Keep the interface quiet around the product: restrained surfaces, strong contrast, and selective color accents let packaging work speak first.

### Color Philosophy
A warm mineral base (#f4f1eb) makes the site feel tactile and premium, like paper stock and substrate samples. Graphite (#1b2024) supplies technical seriousness. Cobalt blue (#173c75) owns the brand and signals repeatable performance. Saffron orange (#e4932d) is used sparingly for motion, highlights, and conversion cues so the brand feels energetic but not loud.

### Layout Paradigm
An editorial field guide rather than a centered marketing grid. Sections alternate between full-bleed image bands, offset text columns, numbered process rails, and horizontal product strips. The contact module is intentionally compact and anchored to the right, mirroring a manufacturing specification sheet.

### Signature Elements
- Fine cobalt measurement rules with small orange index numbers.
- A recurring “V / P” folded-film motif used as a subtle corner mark and section divider.
- Material chips that look like substrate swatches, with the structure name set in compact uppercase type.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a well-made sample book: tactile, immediate, and informative. Hover states reveal a small amount of extra specification rather than dramatic effects. Buttons use short, decisive language. Product tabs update the visual and specification panel without route changes.

### Animation
Use restrained in-view reveals: opacity plus a 14–20px upward transform, staggered by 50ms. Let the hero image drift by 1–2% on scroll only when reduced motion is not requested. Product cards should lift 4px and reveal a saffron index line on hover. Navigation should become a compact frosted surface after scrolling. Keep all UI transitions between 160–260ms with an ease-out curve.

### Typography System
Display: Fraunces, 600–700, for human, editorial headlines and short statements. Body: Manrope, 400–700, for technical clarity and B2B readability. Use compact uppercase labels at 11–12px with 0.14em tracking. Body copy stays between 16–18px with 1.65 line-height. Avoid all-caps paragraphs and avoid generic rounded UI styling.

### Brand Essence
Positioning: A flexible laminated packaging partner for Indian brands that need print fidelity, dependable conversion, and production-ready performance in one clear workflow.
Personality: precise, assured, material-led.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound direct and composed, with a quiet sense of capability. CTAs sound like useful next steps, not hype. Microcopy names what the user will receive.

Example lines:
- “Your pack starts as a specification. We carry it through to a production-ready roll.”
- “Bring us the structure. We’ll bring the control.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark is set in a custom-modified Fraunces treatment: “Vibgyor” in bold serif with a sliced counter on the V, followed by “PRINT N PACK” in compact monospaced capitals. The symbol is a three-band folded-film mark in cobalt, saffron, and graphite; it should appear at a visible 30–36px size, never as a tiny decorative dot.

### Signature Brand Color
Vibgyor Cobalt — #173C75. It appears on rules, critical labels, data callouts, and conversion buttons as the ownable anchor of the system.

## Style Decisions
- Prefer editorial asymmetry over generic centered layouts.
- Use generated industrial and product imagery in the hero, product, factory, and gallery modules.
- No gradients that read as “tech startup”; any tonal shift should come from photography, paper texture, or subtle surface treatment.
- Keep the primary conversion action visible in the header and repeated near the contact module.
